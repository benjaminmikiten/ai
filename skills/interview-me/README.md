# interview-me

`/skills:interview-me` as a plugin, or `/interview-me` with `setup.sh`.

Part of a two-skill writing pipeline with [draft-post](../draft-post):

```
topic → interview-me → interview source doc → draft-post → post draft → your edits
```

A journalist-style interview. It asks one short question at a time, follows the most interesting thread in each answer, and pushes on vague claims, passing mentions, strong opinions and jargon. It doesn't suggest answers; your voice is the product.

It knows it's done from a coverage checklist (hook, why it matters, specifics, point of view, takeaway) plus extra needs that depend on whether the material is shaping up as an essay, a narrative or a guide. It always closes by asking what it should have asked.

The export is a markdown source document: a lightly edited transcript plus interviewer's notes (candidate hooks, near-verbatim pull quotes, thesis, story beats, thin spots, facts to verify, and voice notes) written so a drafter who wasn't in the room can work from it alone.

**Setup:** none. If you keep posts in a repo or vault, it saves interviews to an `interviews/` folder there.
