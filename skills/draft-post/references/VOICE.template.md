## Choosing a register

| Register | Use when the core of the material is… | Test |
|---|---|---|
| **Essay** | A *claim* that generalizes | Can the point be stated in one sentence without "this one time"? |
| **Narrative** | An *experience* where the sequence and the turn are the point | Is there a scene with a surprise or a turn in it? |
| **Guide** | Something the reader will *do* | Will the reader follow along with tools in hand? |
